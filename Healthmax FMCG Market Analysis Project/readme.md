📌 Project Overview
-------
In the high-volume, low-margin world of Fast-Moving Consumer Goods (FMCG), maintaining market leadership requires rigorous data optimization.

In this project, I took on the role of Category Manager for HealthMax, a fictional market leader in the competitive shampoo industry. The objective was to synthesize external retailer data with internal financials to uncover strategic growth opportunities. By applying the principles of Net Revenue Management (NRM), I evaluated pricing, promotional mechanics, and new product pipelines to deliver actionable recommendations that maximize Net Sales and Gross Profit.

📈 Key Methodologies
---------
Time-Series KPI Engineering: Built dynamic models using SUMIFS to calculate continuous YTD and MAT metrics, stripping away seasonality to reveal true underlying market trends.

Profitability Modeling: Reconciled raw financial data, standardized missing decimal values, and calculated Net Sales, Total Gross Profit, and SKU-level Gross Margins.

Promotional Baseline Modeling: Estimated baseline sales (averaging pre- and post-promo months) to isolate pure revenue "Uplift" and calculate the true ROI of trade marketing campaigns.


💡 Strategic Insights & Recommendations
-------
Through rigorous data analysis, I delivered the following insights to the HealthMax executive team:

Competitor Capitalization: Tracked a 4% MAT-to-YTD market share drop for our biggest competitor, FreshCo, following their shift to a digital-only ad strategy. Recommendation: Maintain omnichannel presence to absorb their lost market share.

The Profit Engine vs. The Volume Driver: Identified that while Starbust Ultra Soft drives physical volume (1.15M units), the Shinez Repair range is the true profit engine, operating at a peak 71.67% Gross Margin. Recommendation: Protect the Shinez supply chain aggressively.

Promotional ROI: Proved that deep discounting (e.g., "20% off") yielded a sub-10% ROI, whereas volume-driving mechanics ("Buy 2, Get 1 Free") generated a staggering 1,161% ROI and $7.8M in uplift. Recommendation: Shift Q3/Q4 trade spend entirely to volume-based mechanics.

Product Launch Strategy: Modeled the profitability of two pipeline products. Recommendation: Launch 'Herbashine'. Despite projecting slightly lower topline Net Sales, its lower COGS ($0.90) and higher estimated market share (12%) will generate a significantly stronger overall Gross Profit.
